### Quotes, commitment and customer remedies

The initial paid estimate is a reviewed customer document, not a predicted finished campaign. It must contain exact amount/currency, scope and uncertainties, applicable tax/total, rights, proposed dates, acceptance/version and the required cancellation schedule. An operator must enter the actual quoted terms before checkout; do not infer missing commercial clauses from a model or benchmark.

After payment, procurement gathers genuine voluntary creator quotes. Each quote captures platform, format/count, duration/sequence where relevant, production/travel, rights, dates/timezone, original notes, currency, supplier tax status, expiry and accepted version. Customer acceptance freezes the proposed roster and retail allocations before booking. Creator acceptance freezes the separate Infame–creator agreement. Only their combination plus current readiness/payment/capacity/economics permits commitment. A supplier quote and customer approval are different records.

The customer must approve each replacement. Same-price replacement preserves the customer's accepted retail price but records the new scope/identity and corresponding creator agreement. An upgrade/requote creates a new version and explicit customer approval; payment collection remains independently confirmed. Failure to procure within the approved scope does not grant permission to spend more or deliver less.

Two distinct remedy paths apply:

- **Infame inability to fulfil:** full refund before any creator commitment. After partial commitment/performance, refund undelivered **agreed customer retail value**. Supplier cancellation cost does not reduce this remedy merely because it is confidential or already payable. Record any usable delivered component and its rights under the agreed allocation; no invented delivery credit.
- **Customer-requested cancellation:** each quote must specify explicit, noncumulative **total retained liability** at before-commitment, booked, draft, approved and published/completed stages. Before commitment is zero. Later amounts are quote inputs; no universal percentages were approved. Amounts must be monotonic, use the quote currency, and stay within the corresponding quoted retail item/net order amount. Apply the reached stage once, not booked+draft+approved added together. Do not double-charge delivered value or add retroactive fees.

Shared service charges require their own disclosed allocation and must not break the total cap. Separate net value, VAT adjustment and gross refund exactly; cumulative refunds cannot exceed the paid refundable amount. Terms need legal/tax review for live use and cannot override applicable customer rights. Completion, cancellation, refund and creator payment remain separate state transitions.

### Economics, cash and the five-campaign pilot

Forecast margin is `(VAT-exclusive expected campaign revenue − all direct campaign costs) / VAT-exclusive expected campaign revenue`. Use exact minor units and rational comparisons, not floating-point thresholds. A25% markup is not a25% margin. At SAR5,000 net revenue, total direct cost must not exceed SAR3,750 for the25% floor or SAR3,500 for the30% target. These are illustrative arithmetic, not market rates.

Capture quoted/committed/actual supplier and production cost, loaded staff time, gateway/FX charges where applicable, operational AI, rework allowance and nonrecoverable tax. Reforecast before each booking, replacement or additional expense; customer refunds reduce expected revenue. Do not assume all input VAT is recoverable. Revenue recognition and deferred advances need reviewed accounting treatment; receiving cash does not make the whole campaign completed revenue.

Maintain an actual cash/liability schedule for customer refunds, supplier obligations, tax, disputes and settlement delays. Real founder-funded loss/refund capacity is required before live billing. A later customer's deposit is not an assumed source of funding for an earlier loss. Stop new admissions/commitments when the verified funds or forecast fail; preserve already accepted obligations and use the agreed fulfilment/refund process. The pilot cap applies across operators and agencies, with transactional admission/release and genuine saved active states. A merely opened estimate/planning draft is not an active campaign; reserve a slot before checkout. Unknown payment execution keeps that reservation held until reconciliation; fulfilment/cancellation releases operational capacity only after a recorded resolution and does not erase financial liabilities.

Record estimated versus actual labor, creative rounds, procurement failure, replacements, refunds, contribution and time-to-delivery for each pilot campaign. Five simultaneous campaigns and target margins are operating limits, not traction. Reassess the estimate range and capacity after genuine fulfilled campaigns; do not quietly change accepted quote versions.
