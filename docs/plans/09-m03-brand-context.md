## Full customer flow

Create/edit an optional private named brand context: positioning, values, tone,
reference text/HTTPS URLs, competitor names/notes and original language metadata.
Blank context and no website are supported. Save original words, review, then
explicitly approve a version for planning. Approval is not factual/legal verification.
Edit/archiving never rewrites or deletes retained approved versions.

Choose one exact approved version or explicitly choose no context in the retained
campaign form. Preview context separately; selecting never replaces dirty fields.
Optional reuse offers only semantically exact supported fields (initially tone),
with visible keep/replace review and explicit application. Other context remains
human planning input, not a guessed campaign summary, budget, audience or goal.
Save free manual form requirements and durable unprocessed chat/paragraph text;
no claimed extraction or assistant activity while the provider is unavailable.
Generate a new brief, review its exact selected-context provenance, and obtain
the actual current Brand owner's explicit brief approval. Historical direct deals
continue to use their original accepted brief/parties regardless of later context.

References are private scoped data. Plain text/URLs work without import/storage.
No arbitrary URL fetch or imported instructions execute tools/approval. Actual
reference-file upload/download needs a new declared marketing-reference purpose
and exact context-scope authorization; old proof purposes cannot be repurposed.
That bounded asset integration is required before offering file controls or claiming
the full reference-asset acceptance. External private-storage readiness stays honest.

## Scope, version and persistence freeze to review

Context and new planning scopes are immutable OWN_BRAND (brandId, null org/client)
or CLIENT (exact brandId/org/client composite FK). Same Brand does not substitute
another client. Absence of a campaign planning binding remains legacy owner scope;
an agency cannot infer access to an old campaign from its Brand.

Own company reads/writes prove the native current Brand owner. Agency CLIENT reads
require view and writes plan via the existing same-client atomic authority callback. Agency
ownership and all other saved capabilities confer no implicit approval. Context
approval and brief approval remain actual Brand-owner actions. A CLIENT plan writer
explicitly submits an immutable exact context/brief review snapshot to the captured
current Brand owner. A separate owner-review inbox/detail/command lane checks that
target, current Brand owner/session version and exact currently active association.
It reveals only submitted review content and pinned provenance, not unsubmitted
agency drafts, narrative or other client records. No agency membership/grant or actor
substitution is manufactured. Later editing cannot replace the submitted snapshot;
stale input/selection/source review requires explicit resubmission. Owner transfer,
authorization-version drift or association withdrawal blocks fresh approval.
Plan-only replies/replays contain minimal revisions/IDs only, never private content.

Add an immutable PlanningWorkspace scope binding, unique per own Brand or exact
CLIENT. Context and campaign bindings carry its nonnull ID; composite same-Brand
Campaign/Workspace FKs and same-Workspace Context/Version/Selection FKs prevent
nullable CLIENT columns from bypassing a same-scope relationship. Add draft-context
parents, immutable owner-review submissions and immutable approved BrandContextVersion snapshots
with original content, input revision, positive version, actual approval actor/time
and canonical hash. Retain referenced versions. Add campaign immutable scope,
separate versioned selected-context pointer/history and durable narrative storage;
keep all of them outside strict Campaign.input. Add append-only per-new-brief
provenance referencing its exact selected version/hash (or explicit no-context).
Selection history has immutable identity and monotonically increasing revision,
including explicit NONE; genuinely absent legacy selection stays distinct. Brief
provenance pins exact selection identity/revision, not just version/hash. Composite
brief/campaign and selection/campaign/Workspace FKs prevent substituted provenance.
Context-reuse proposals have a separate immutable dependency row binding proposal,
campaign, fieldsRevision and selection identity/revision, rejecting A→B→A. Ordinary
human proposals are separately classified. Old briefs have absent provenance;
no invented backfill.

Add dedicated metadata-only scoped context/planning journals keyed actor/UUID/hash,
with exact scope/resource/action/revisions and no content/conversation/contacts.
Current authorization and exact scope binding precede historical replay. Replay may
bypass old revisions only; changed key payload or revoked permission rejects.
Fresh writes compare expected parent/campaign/selection and authority revisions.
Selection changes advance campaign state, invalidate current approval and stale
context-dependent proposals, without altering any old approved brief/agreement.

Extract DB-only mutation/generation cores from current campaign bodies. Legacy
wrappers retain their schemas/hashes/receipts/results and actual owner check. Every
API wrapper passes trusted server session metadata outside input/hash and checks
the native current/final session, including replay. Fresh legacy writes to an
explicitly bound new OWN/CLIENT campaign reject SCOPED_PLANNING_REQUIRED after
authorized historical replay; they cannot bypass selection or new journals. Existing
unbound campaigns keep the old flow until explicit owned binding/selection. No
legacy Context lock is needed on that denial path. Bound CLIENT campaigns are
excluded from legacy owner lists/default selectors and full GET; specific legacy
reads return unavailable, never fall back to another campaign. This includes SSR
brief/matches selectors and dependent legacy matching/direct-offer projections,
which must not reveal unsubmitted CLIENT fields/conversation/briefs or turn a
submitted review into general agency access. Only new scoped agency view or exact
submitted owner-review projections expose content. Existing old campaigns cannot
be rebound as CLIENT; CLIENT creation is new/explicit and has no old owner receipts.
OWN explicit binding may retain genuine pre-binding owner history unchanged. New
CLIENT namespace composes cores inside withClientAuthority; no nested transaction,
late User lock or old owner journal write. New brief reuse/owner approval compares
exact current selected-context provenance in addition to original field gates.
Context archive blocks new context-version submission/approval, selection and
context-reuse proposals. It does not block a campaign already pinned to an approved
version from generating, submitting or approving its brief under current authority
and exact provenance; that established snapshot remains usable/readable. Archive
never rewrites a brief or relinks to latest. Current
authorization/exact scope precedes replay; exact history bypasses fresh archive/
revision predicates only. File deletion/unavailability is shown honestly and never
fabricates proof or permits public asset use.
Use one documented ordered native User/session/authority/Brand/Context/Campaign/
selection/version lock order and bounded reads/audits/final authorization.
